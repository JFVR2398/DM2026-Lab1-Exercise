This report is for documenting the process of analyzing a REDDIT post dataset using the created tools in the Development section.

First, load_dataset_tool was loaded with these parameters: path = 'newdataset/Reddit-stock-sentiment.csv', text_column="text", label_column="label". It loaded 847 documents. The three categories loaded with their counts are as follow: category -1.0 (negative) with 315 documents, 0.0 (neutral) with 423 documents, 1.0 (positive) with 109 documents (result_id: load_dataset_1). Then the first 5 rows were inspected using inspect_data_tool (result_id: inspect_data_2). There were no missing datapoints found using check_missing_tool (result_id: check_missing_3).

When the check_duplicates_tool was used, 34 documents where found as duplicated (result_id: check_duplicates_4). Because these represent only 4% of the total dataset, it was decided to drop them for the analysis as it would not have a relevant impapct in the sample. For reaching that conclusion, the indices were manuale evaluated and it was found they had incomplete information or they were blanks. After dropping the duplicates, a total of 813 documents were left in the dataset for the next steps (result_id: check_duplicates_5).

The describe_data_tool allowed to retrieve summary and descriptive statistics of the dataset. The mean length of the documents is 147.1857 with a standard deviation of 239.0092 (result_id: describe_data_6). After inspecting the descriptive data by category, it was found the three of them have similar behaviour. Only the category 1.0 (positive) has the least amount of documents (107) compared to the other two. Categories-1.0 negative has a total of 315 documents and 0.0 neutral has 391 documents (result_id: describe_data_6). In the boxplot it can be observed the amount of outliers, which are similar between categories -1.0 and 0.0.
